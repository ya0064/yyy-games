关于此游戏

Incursion Red River is a fully PvE tactical cooperative extraction shooter based in a de-stabilized and immersive modern-day Vietnam. As a Private Military Contractor you must deal with 3 factions that stand to gain from the chaos of a war-torn country- you will engage in ruthless combat, discover dark secrets, and play to your advantage as the progression and story unfolds.

ENTER THE JUNGLE

Incursion Red River lets you engage with up to 3 of your friends as squadmates throughout a destabilized Vietnam. Complete Operations and Tasks from 3 diverse factions throughout multiple detailed maps to increase your reputation and rewards. Shoot and loot your way through immersive and deadly AI encounters in the tropics to gain influence and wealth, or die trying and lose it all.

PLAN AHEAD FOR YOUR DEPLOYMENT

Incursion Red River provides the player with a balanced first-person experience that feels realistic, yet fun. Equipment and weapons influence ergonomics and combat playstyle, so you will need to pick your loadout carefully. Access the Marketplace to purchase limited quantities of weapons, medical equipment, gear, and ammunition. Whether you prefer to be scoped from afar or keeping things close and personal, loud or quiet, day or night, rain or shine- you choose how you get the task done. Simulated ballistic systems affect weapons and have unique characteristics that can be adjusted with attachments. Bringing in the right gear, having a proper plan of attack, and emphasizing tactical plays will maximize the success rate for deployments.

CUSTOMIZE TO YOUR OWN STYLE

You have the ability to select and fully customize a wide variety of weapons and gear. Swap out barrels, handguards, foregrips, stocks, sights or magazines and turn your trusty Kalashnikov from a close-quarters menace, into a deadly accurate long range rifle. Utilize the Gunsmith table to save &#038; load your favorite presets.

However, the customizable experience doesn’t stop at your loadout. Play to your strengths or call out your weaknesses by adjusting or fine tuning AI difficulty at any point to ensure you always have the right level of challenge.

INVESTIGATE AND SURVIVE

After a 10 year war, the People’s Liberation Army repels invasion in Vietnam and through continued Soviet backing, arms manufacturing and exporting prosper; along with the country as a whole.

20 years later the Soviet Union collapses. Vietnam seeks a source of stabilization, hesitantly finding it in the United States, who soon takes it too far. In response, China funds a political party in their image, the Vietnamese Communist Union Party (VCUP), leading to the old government of Vietnam being overthrown in a coup.

The less-than-fair election sparks a full civil war. China allies with Russia to repel the West, sending in the Iron Guard Corps (IGC). Western nations send “humanitarian aid” via United Intercontinental Security (UICS), and sick of endless imperialism, the Vietnamese Liberation Front (VLF) rises among the local population to free Vietnam.

The civil war soon dissolves into a faction war. A mysterious black-ops corporation known as FANG sends YOU to the country to investigate the factions for a hidden microwave weapon, last located in the Red River Delta region.

YOU will be contracted by each faction to urge closer to finding the weapon while unearthing dishonest motivations and making difficult calls.

MAKE CONTACT WITH THE LOCALS

UNITED INTERCONTINENTAL SECURITY (UICS)

While some members offer aid and relief to the wartorn Vietnam, others have found destroying communism to be a more worthy goal. Funded by a large corporation, NATO holds the strings, using the UICS to expand its influence.

VIETNAMESE LIBERATION FRONT (VLF)

The growing movement of veterans, rebels, and old-government resources means the VLF has become a serious threat. VLF strategic, violent raids continue to succeed against invading factions and traitorous locals alike.

IRON GUARD CORPS (IGC)

The IGC, formed by China and Russia, fights to prop up a sympathetic government. Better for Vietnam to be supported by neighbors than the capitalist West. In order to avoid a full world war the IGC must balance military action and deniability.
