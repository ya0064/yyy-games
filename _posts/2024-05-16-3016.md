游戏发行：Green Grass Games

发售日期：2022-03-12

【游戏介绍】

致命耀斑是一款末日背景开放世界生存制作类动作冒险游戏，一场无情的太阳耀斑风暴让生物变成了残暴的行尸，剩下的野兽和匪徒也威胁着你的生存。探索广阔的灾后世界，采集资源，制作工具，建造基地，布置陷阱，在无序的新世界努力存活下去。

Explore a vast open world filled with many building and areas. Explore distinct places with special items to loot and fight the bloodthirsty monsters or the armed bandits.

Hunt animals for food and defend yourself from natural predators such as Bears and Wolves

Defeat the bandits and take their loot

Use your guns and traps to protect your base and yourself from the undead hordes

Explore mines and the forest to gather natural resources like special minerals and wood to build survival tools and structures

Build a base and tools to survive in this new world
